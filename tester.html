Installing

  • Windows 10 or 11: run the setup file. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
  • Android 10 or later: download the APK on your phone and open it; allow installs from your browser when asked.

Don't have a code? The free demo (the Prologue and Act I) is always there, and its save carries over to the full game.
