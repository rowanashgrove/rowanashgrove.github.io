Public-domain assets · CC0

No credit is required for these, but they are listed anyway.

  • Quaternius: Universal Base Characters, Universal Animation Library 1 and 2, and the low-poly animals used in the painted look
  • MakeHuman community: the bodies, skins, eyes, hair and beards behind every villager, Rowan and the apprentice (CC0 asset packs, made with MPFB 2 in Blender)
  • Poly Haven: rocks, ferns, stumps, the lantern, crates, barrels, and stone, roof, wood, bark, grass and cloth textures
  • ambientCG: leaf and grass textures
  • OpenGameArt sound: Spring Spring, PagDev, rubberduck, Fantozzi, TinyWorlds, Kenney, JaggedStone, congusbongus and AntumDeluge for ambience, footsteps, interface and magic sounds

Made for the game

  • The whole musical score, composed and synthesised for Ashgrove
  • Spell effects, robes, masks, the sky, water, fire and mist, all made in code
  • Story, world and characters from Rowan Ashgrove’s books, The Fivefold Grimoire and Ashgrove’s Origins
