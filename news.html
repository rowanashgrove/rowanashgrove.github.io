24 November 20261.4.2

Version 1.4.2: a smoother start

A small update to the loading screen. The world used to be built in a few big pieces, and the loading screen froze for five or six seconds at a time while it waited. It is now built a little at a time, so the painting and the music keep moving all the way to the title. The village houses, the people of the Reach, the mere’s bank stones and Rowan himself are now made in small steps, and the world’s first sight of its own colours happens behind the loading screen instead of in one long stall. Loading is also a little quicker. Saves carry over. (1.4.2 replaces 1.4.1, which could crash on the loading screen on some PCs.)

24 November 20261.4

Version 1.4: Act IV, The Wide Sky

The story goes up onto the high moor. Cross the shepherds’ road to Carn Hollow, learn Sowilo, the sun rune, face the gale-wraiths and climb the Star Tower for the second of the twelve keys.

  • Earn the robe. The apprentice is a young man who walks up to the house in his own village clothes; Rowan gives him the robe at the hearth.
  • Robes that move. Rowan and the apprentice rebuilt in higher detail, with robes that swing as you walk and run, in quieter, more lifelike wool with runes stitched in bronze thread.
  • A new score. Recorded pieces for the house, the village, the Whisperwood, the night, the mere, combat and the Echo.
  • Footsteps that follow your feet on grass, leaf litter, paths, stone, boards and shallow water.
  • An animated title and loading screen, and smoother frame rates on the same settings.

Your saves carry over. The new music is by Alexandr Zhelanov, Matthew Pablo, Hitctrl, VWolfdog, RandomMind and cynicmusic; see the credits page.

18 November 2026Books

The books, revised

Three of the Fivefold Grimoire books have new editions on Kindle, with corrections readers would have tripped over in practice.

  • The Unbroken Thread: the worked example in the Abundance Sigil now shows the right letters.
  • The Waking Vessel: the Doctrine of Three Notes now matches the seven oil recipes: lavender, not chamomile, as the Voice for quiet, and no ginger or resin listed in the wrong note.
  • The Tended Flame: the Cut Wick now says to cut just below the flame, the way that actually puts it out safely.

The new editions are rolling out on Kindle over the next few days. If you already own a book, Amazon will offer the update in your Kindle library once it is live.

18 November 20261.3

Version 1.3: the Reach, made real

The biggest change yet. The whole Reach now has a realistic look: weathered stone and slate, grass that moves in the wind, real trees, and skies that turn from morning haze to gold to a sky full of stars.

  • Real animals. Horses, cows, sheep and a donkey in the pasture, pigs in the pen, hens on the green, foxes in the meadow and wolves in the Whisperwood at night.
  • Rowan Ashgrove, remade. A new face, a gilded half-mask, and one full runed robe with runes round the hood.
  • The people of Hollowmere have new skin detail and eyes that catch the light.
  • A new icon on PC and phone.
  • Prefer the painted look? Turn off “Realistic look” in Settings.

The realistic animals are CC BY models; their makers are named on the credits page. Your saves carry over.

Version 1.2: a Reach that lives without you

  • Weather: rain, storms with thunder, and mist off the mere.
  • Fishing from the jetty on Stillwater Mere: six fish, some only after dark or under a full moon.
  • Benches that mend you and let the hours pass.
  • Companions: Tom or Ada will walk the Reach with you once you are friends.
  • Bonds: remember what each villager loves and bring it to them.
  • The undercroft beneath the house, and two new shades, the Cinders and the Hollowed.
  • Spoken voices for the people of the Reach, and story cutscenes.
  • Three save slots, 16 achievements and New Game+.

7 November 20261.1

Version 1.1: Android, magic circles and a dodge roll

Ashgrove now runs on Android as well as Windows, and one purchase covers both. The free demo is on Android too.

  • Every working is cast through its own circle of runes that draws itself in light.
  • Combat: auto-aim, a dodge roll, red warnings before heavy attacks, and health bars on every shade.
  • Your robe grows with you: every working you learn stitches more gold runes into it.
  • Save anywhere, a quest marker, photo mode, and a full set of settings: difficulty, remappable keys, text size, field of view, and a touch layout you can rearrange.
